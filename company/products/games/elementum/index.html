ELEMENTUMstep 5 — town life, packs of monsters and other players

Your math level (the forge's problems start here and then adapt — right on the first try makes them harder, stuck makes them easier):

Computer: WASD walk, Shift run, Space jump, mouse looks. Click uses what is in your hand. 1–9 hotbar, E bag & forge, T trade at the stall, R reload, Q crystal. Phone: joystick, drag to look, FIRE, tap the hotbar, 🎒 bag.

You start in Oppidum with nothing — just your fists. Punch or chop trees 🌳 for wood and grey rocks for iron. Villagers with a ❗ need help and pay you in coins 🪙. Walk up to things and press T: Mercator 🪙 sells your first gun and buys what you find, the Popina 🍞 sells food, and your house 🏠 (your name over the door; lock it so only you can get in) has a bed and a chest. Build the training center 🔨 to practise against dummies that shoot back.

Other players show up with their name tags. Go out through a gate into the wilds: nine kinds of monsters roam in packs of four (a chief and three followers) with their own spells, glowing crystal veins and iron rocks to mine. Health only comes back from healing spells and potions. The forge hides every recipe in math problems — cores are numbered 🟠 2 · 🔵 3 · 🟤 5 · ⚪ 7 · 🟡 11.
