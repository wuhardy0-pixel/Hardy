# Elemental Latin

Status: **live** at play.hardywu.com/play/latin (sign-in at loglatin.hardywu.com).

Superpowers with Latin names — and the spell only works when the Latin is right. Phone-first. Files: `index.html` (the Latin rules, the spell builder, the HUD), `scene.js` (the 3D world, arena and spell effects), `three.min.js` (Three.js r158, vendored), `art/` (hero cards + biome textures, built by `tools/build_art.py`); the server serves them at /play/latin/<path>.

How it plays
- **3D, third-person** (Fortnite-style camera behind your hero): the world is 3D terrain built from the tile map and faced with the pack's cube textures (moss, petrified wood, pond, mountain stone, lava rock, glacier ice, lunar stone, salt crystal, city brick, granite, sun-bleached wood), with forests, ice spikes, volcanoes that burn with a real flame shader, towns, arena pillars and your camp fire. Heroes and rival mages are **portrait cards standing in the world** (cut-out paintings with a glowing element ring, a shadow and a floating orb) that always face the camera; creatures are 3D beasts. Battles happen in an arena floored with the biome's texture. **Every spell is an effect**: fire sprays embers, water arcs and splashes, lightning cracks as a jagged bolt, earth lobs rocks, wind spirals, ice shards, light fires a beam, shadow smokes; shields are glowing domes that shatter when broken; heals sparkle green; a charge makes the staff orb swell; a wrong ending puffs grey smoke; a tame call sends gold rings at the creature and, when it works, it shrinks into a gold orb that flies to you. Hits flinch the target, shake the camera and pop a damage number; being hit flashes the screen edge red. The Ludus Royale has a purple storm wall that shrinks after each win.
- **A world to walk** (Prodigy-style): a tile map with ember fields (fire), a lake (water), deep forest (air/earth), thunder peaks (lightning/earth), frost tundra (ice), shadow woods, a light shrine, towns and the arena. D-pad, tap-to-walk or arrow keys. Wild creatures of each biome jump out as you walk, or press Search.
- **Your hero** (Fortnite-style skins): you play as one of 12 painted heroes — Windy Wizard first, then Forest Druid, Fire Lord, Mermaid Knight, Dwarf Count, Lady of the Lightning Storm, Obsidian Elf King, Ice Queen, Wind Lord, Phoenix Pharaoh, Vampire Princess of the Snow, Nebula Nymph — unlocked along a **reward track** by XP, plus hats; the **Royale crown** only for winning a Ludus Royale. The look is remembered on the device.
- **Ludus Royale**: six mages enter, the ring shrinks after every duel, your health carries over, last one standing wins the crown.
- **Towns**: the local mage challenges you — turn-based duels against twelve human rivals, each with their own portrait (Marcus, Livia, Cassius, Quintus, Flavia, Drusilla, Gaius, Aurelius, Octavia, Glaucus, Lupa, Magister Umbrarum) who cast their own Latin spells, shown with translations. **The wild**: creatures (Salamandra, Hydra, Gryphus, Ursus, Lupus, Draco, Phoenix, Felis Umbrae) you weaken and then **tame** by calling their name in the **vocative** plus the command *veni!* (Gryphus → Gryphe, veni!). Knock one out and it flees; tame chance rises as its health falls. Tamed creatures are your familiars; pick the active one at camp. Elements beat each other in a ring (fire → ice → air → earth → lightning → water → fire; light ↔ shadow).
- You build each spell from four parts: an element noun (ignis, aqua, terra, ventus, fulmen, glacies, lux, umbra), its **case ending**, a verb (iacio throw, voco summon, sano heal, frango break, augeo strengthen, pugno fight), and its **person ending**. "ignem iacio" = I throw fire (attack); "aquam voco" = I summon water (shield); "me sano" = I heal myself; "igne pugno" = I fight with fire (ablative, level 5).
- Wrong ending → the spell fizzles and the game explains the rule (why accusative, why -o for "I", what the imperative is for). Right ending → full power, XP, streak bonus.
- Levels (by XP) unlock words, creatures, persons and cases: the imperative commands your familiar (iace!), level 3 lets you cast as "we" (-mus) for a double strike, level 5 adds the ablative.
- **Spellbook**: any correct spell can be kept as a named skill (stored in the browser) and recast with one tap. **Codex** (📖) shows the tables learned so far.

Controls: tap the parts, Cast (or Enter), Hint, 📖 codex, ☰ pause (P/Esc). 💬 feedback button. No sign-in code in the game.
