Elemental LatinSuperpowers with Latin names. The spell only works if the Latin is right.

Fire is ignis, water is aqua, lightning is fulmen. To throw fire you need the object form: ignem iacio — "I throw fire". Get an ending wrong and the spell fizzles, but the game tells you why.

Beat the creatures, level up, unlock new words, persons and cases. Forge your own skills and keep them in your spellbook.
