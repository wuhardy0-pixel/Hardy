Elemental LatinElemental powers with Latin names. The spell only works if the Latin is right.

Fire is ignis, water is aqua, lightning is fulmen. To throw fire you need the object form: ignem iacio — "I throw fire". Get an ending wrong and the spell fizzles, but the game tells you why.

Walk the world: ember fields, frost tundra, thunder peaks, shadow woods. Weaken wild creatures and tame them by calling their name in the vocative — Gryphe, veni!. Duel the mage of each town. Enter the Ludus Royale, where six mages fight until one stands. Earn XP, climb the reward track, unlock outfits and the crown, and keep your own skills in a spellbook.
