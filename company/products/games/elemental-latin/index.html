Elemental LatinElemental powers with Latin names. The spell only works if the Latin is right.

Fire is ignis, water is aqua, lightning is fulmen. To throw fire you need the object form: ignem iacio — "I throw fire". Get an ending wrong and the spell fizzles, but the game tells you why.

Thirty heroes to play as — the Windy Wizard first, then the Fire Lord, the Ice Queen, the Obsidian Elf King and more as you earn XP. A 3D world you walk through: ember fields, frost tundra, thunder peaks, shadow woods. Every spell you get right sprays real fire, water or lightning across the arena. Weaken wild creatures and tame them by calling their name in the vocative — Gryphe, veni!. Duel the mage of each town. Enter the Ludus Royale, where six mages fight until one stands. Earn XP, climb the reward track, unlock outfits and the crown, and keep your own skills in a spellbook.
