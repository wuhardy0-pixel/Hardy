# Twisty Blep Ball

Hardy's own design, made from scratch on 2026-10-31 (it is not based on anyone else's files). Barbara: change anything freely.

- **Sell this?** yes, once the first test print works
- **Price:** $13.99
- **Colours:** the body is the customer's colour. The eyes are White, the pupils Black, the tongue Red. That's 4 filaments.
- **Files:** load the four STLs together as one object with parts:
  - ball_body.stl: the customer's colour
  - ball_eyes.stl: White
  - ball_pupils.stl: Black
  - ball_tongue.stl: Red
- **Printing:** stand it upright as it comes, no supports. The 10 twisted ribs are 2.2 mm thick.
- **Material:** try PETG first. PETG bends and springs back better than PLA. TPU would be even squishier.

## First test print, before selling

1. **Squeeze:** press the top down. The ribs should bend and turn the head, then spring back.
2. **Too stiff or too floppy:** if it's too stiff, tell Claude to make the ribs thinner. If it's too floppy, make them thicker.
3. **Photos:** take a few real photos for the shop.
