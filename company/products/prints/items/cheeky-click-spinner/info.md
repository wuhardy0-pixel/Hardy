# Cheeky Click Spinner

Hardy's own design, made from scratch on 2026-10-31 (it is not based on anyone else's files). Barbara: change anything freely.

- **Sell this?** yes, once the first test print works
- **Price:** $13.99
- **Colours:** the body is the customer's colour. The hub and eyes are White, the pupils Black, the tongues Red. That's 4 filaments.
- **Files:** load all five STLs together as one object with parts, and give each part its colour:
  - spinner_body.stl: the customer's colour
  - spinner_hub.stl: White
  - spinner_eyes.stl: White
  - spinner_pupils.stl: Black
  - spinner_tongue.stl: Red
- **Printing:** lay it flat as it comes, no supports, 0.2 mm layers, PLA. The hub prints inside the body with a 0.45 mm gap.

## First test print, before selling

1. **Hub:** after printing, twist the hub firmly once to break it free. It should then spin freely and never fall out.
2. **Clicks:** three thin springy clickers beside the hub tick over 6 bumps. If it doesn't click, or the clickers are too tight, tell Claude the clearance to change.
3. **Photos:** take a few real photos for the shop.

The models are made by make_fidgets.py, so they can be changed with code.
