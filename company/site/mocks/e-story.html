It starts as a sketch.

02

Then a 3D model.

The sketch becomes a model on the computer, sized so the lid clicks shut and the handle holds a full lunch.

03

Printed layer by layer.

The printer lays down hundreds of thin layers of plastic in the colours you chose. The lunchbox takes about 14 hours.
