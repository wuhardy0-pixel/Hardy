BookKeep Lite v9

Parser fixes:
- Month-name dates such as August 12.
- Relative dates: today / yesterday.
- Mixed messages containing sales AND expenses.
- 'put both sales there' resolves to the newly created account.
- Keeps individual sales separate and independently totals them.
- Shows total sales and total expenses separately.
- Retains reinforcement-style learning, duplicate checks, edits, and validation.

Reports:
- Balance Sheet
- Income Statement
- Cash Flow Statement
- Reports use the selected cash/accrual method where applicable.
- Lightweight balance sheet derives equity as Assets - Liabilities.

Users:
- Add multiple users.
- Switch users from the top-right selector.
- Each user has separate accounts, transactions, investments, settings, and learned-agent preferences.

Important:
This is a lightweight bookkeeping app, not a replacement for professional accounting software.
The statements are useful for personal/small-project bookkeeping but do not yet implement a full formal general ledger.
