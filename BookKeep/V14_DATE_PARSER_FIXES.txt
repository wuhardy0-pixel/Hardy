BookKeep Lite v14 — Date and Relative-Time Fixes

Fixed/added:
- 2 days ago / two days ago
- N days ago
- N days from now / later
- today / yesterday / tomorrow
- day before yesterday / day after tomorrow
- last Monday, next Friday, this Tuesday, etc.
- numeric dates: 8/11/2026, 2026-08-11
- month names: August 12, August 12th, August 12, 2026
- basic date validation to prevent past phrases from resolving into the future and vice versa
- transaction summaries now show the resolved date
- Settings includes a Date Parser Test field so relative-date bugs can be checked quickly

The parser still uses the user's local browser date/time as the reference clock.
