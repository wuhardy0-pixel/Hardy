Chart of accounts (the standard account names the agent uses)

Assets — what you own

  • Cash — physical money
  • Accounts Receivable — customers owe you (unpaid invoices)
  • Equipment — things you own to run the business
  • Supplies — materials on hand
  • Investments — stocks you own, at what you paid for them
  • …plus any money accounts you create (e.g. "Earnings", or a bank account like "Checking" someday) — always counted as assets

Liabilities — what you owe

  • Accounts Payable — bills you have not paid yet

Equity — the owner's stake

  • Owner's Equity — what the owner put in; net income joins it on the balance sheet

Revenue — money earned

  • Sales Revenue — selling products (rulers, prints…)
  • Service Revenue — doing work (consulting, tutoring, babysitting…)
  • Interest Income — interest earned
  • Other Income — everything else (bets, gifts, prizes, refunds…)
  • Investment Income — dividends and profits from selling stocks

Expenses — money spent

  • Cost of Goods Sold — the materials inside the products you sold (e.g. 20g of filament in a ruler)
  • R&D Expense — filament/materials spent on tests, failed prints, and experiments
  • Supplies Expense — materials bought and used
  • Rent Expense
  • Utilities Expense — electricity, internet…
  • Wages Expense — paying helpers
  • Other Expense — everything else
  • Investment Loss — selling a stock for less than you paid

The agent picks from these automatically; details like the item or customer go in the note. Never raw item words — a ruler sale is "Sales Revenue", not "ruler".
